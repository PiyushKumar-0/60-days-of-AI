How to Play

Alerts represent live disruptions across your supply chain. Each one shows a priority, a countdown, and its business impact.

  • Pick the best action for each alert — actions like Expedite Shipment or Use Backup Supplier resolve issues but cost money or time.
  • Ignoring or letting an alert expire hurts your KPIs and Score.
  • Delay Decision buys time but the situation may worsen.
  • Some actions have delayed consequences — watch the toast notifications.
  • Balance Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency, Operating Cost, and Revenue Protected to earn the highest Score and Grade.
